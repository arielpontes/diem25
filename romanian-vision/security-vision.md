# Real security for Romania means peace on our borders and resilience at home — not a blank cheque for the arms industry.

*Like every DiEM25 Romania vision document, this one is concrete about direction and deliberately silent on fine detail. Every policy we name already works somewhere in Europe, and we cite the laws so you can verify us. The fully specified, costed Romanian versions will be drafted democratically by our members — and, when the time comes, by candidates who put them before voters.*

Romanians' fear of Russia is not paranoia. It is memory: of Bessarabia taken, of an occupation, of a dictatorship imposed. We do not ask Romanians to forget it, and in line with the [Athens Declaration](https://diem25.org/yanis-varoufakis-jeremy-corbyn-and-ece-temelkuran-present-the-athens-declaration/) we stand with the people of Ukraine as with every people suffering invasion, displacement and aggression.

But fear is a poor procurement officer. Romania has committed to spending ever more of its GDP on weapons — including billions of euros on American fighter jets — while its hospitals, schools and civil protection systems crumble. And a security doctrine that consists of hosting bases and buying whatever Washington sells is not sovereignty: it is dependence on the mood of the American electorate every four years. We view non-aligned, democratic and sovereign nations working together as the route to lasting peace. Our Romania will be:

- **Peaceful,** demanding a ceasefire in every conflict, the withdrawal of occupying forces and a comprehensive peace under the auspices of the United Nations.
- **European,** anchoring its security in cooperation with its EU partners — including the mutual assistance clause of Article 42.7 of the EU Treaty — rather than in exclusive dependence on any superpower.
- **Non-aligned in spirit,** working toward the replacement of competing military blocs with an inclusive international security framework that de-escalates tensions instead of feeding a New Cold War.
- **Resilient,** understanding that a country with strong hospitals, energy independence, functioning institutions and citizens who trust their state is harder to destabilise than any arsenal can make it.

## What we propose

- **Diplomacy first, always.** Romania, as a direct neighbour of the war, should be a loud voice for negotiations, ceasefire and a comprehensive peace with real security guarantees — not a cheerleader for escalation. Peace in Ukraine is not a concession to the aggressor; it is the survival of Ukrainians and the security of Romanians.
- **Learn from Europe's neutrals.** Austria (Neutrality Act of 1955), Ireland (with its 'triple lock' on deploying troops) and Malta (neutrality written into its constitution) are EU members that are safe, prosperous and attacked by no one. We do not pretend Romania's geography is Austria's, and we are honest that any change to Romania's alliances is a long-term question the Romanian people would have to decide democratically. But these countries prove the premise our militarists deny: that security without bloc politics is possible in Europe.
- **Democratic control over military spending.** Full parliamentary and public scrutiny of every major procurement contract — no more multi-billion-euro purchases negotiated over the heads of citizens. Every leu spent on weapons is a leu not spent on the hospitals and schools whose absence drives Romanians out of the country; that trade-off must be argued in public, not assumed.
- **Invest in civilian resilience.** Treat energy independence, cybersecurity, civil protection, public health and social cohesion as the core of national security — because they are. A depopulated, distrustful country is insecure no matter how advanced its fighter jets.
- **Ban the weapons of the next war.** Support the international prohibition of autonomous AI-based weapons, and join Austria, Ireland and Malta in supporting the UN Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons.

We are told that anyone who questions militarism is a Kremlin asset. We answer: the surest gift to every authoritarian regime is a Europe that abandons diplomacy, and a Romania too busy buying weapons to keep its people healthy, educated and home.
