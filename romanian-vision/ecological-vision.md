# Romania's mountains, forests, rivers and soil belong to the people who live here — not to the foreign corporations that mine, log and drill them.

*Like every DiEM25 Romania vision document, this one is concrete about direction and deliberately silent on fine detail. Every policy we name already works somewhere in Europe, and we cite the laws so you can verify us. The fully specified, costed Romanian versions will be drafted democratically by our members — and, when the time comes, by candidates who put them before voters.*

Remember Roșia Montană. A foreign mining corporation spent years trying to blast open a mountain with cyanide, and when the Romanian people stopped it in the streets, the corporation sued Romania for billions before a private arbitration tribunal — and lost. Remember that the Carpathians hold most of the EU's remaining virgin forests, and that they have been cut, wagon by wagon, to feed Austrian-owned sawmills and the supply chains of IKEA, Romania's largest private forest owner, while foresters who resisted were beaten and killed. Remember that the gas under the Black Sea is being developed by a company majority-owned in Vienna. The pattern is always the same: our resources, their profits, our pollution.

We stand with DiEM25's [Green New Deal for Europe](https://gnde.diem25.org): the ecological transition is not a sacrifice to be endured but the greatest opportunity in a generation — if its costs fall on those who profited from destruction, not on the weakest. Our Romania will be:

- **Sovereign over its resources,** ending the colonial arrangement in which Romania exports raw logs, ore and gas and imports back the finished products and the environmental damage.
- **Sane,** refusing to sacrifice what remains of our natural heritage to keep dividends flowing to foreign shareholders.
- **Just,** guaranteeing that no Romanian has to choose between heating and eating — in a country with some of the EU's worst energy poverty.
- **Responsible,** treating the climate crisis with the urgency the science demands, not the urgency the lobbyists permit.

## What we propose

- **Democratic control over natural resources.** Renegotiate royalties and concessions for minerals, timber and hydrocarbons so that the public captures a fair share of resource wealth, with public stakes in strategic projects — and oppose the investor-state dispute (ISDS) clauses that let corporations like the one behind Roșia Montană sue peoples for daring to say no.
- **Water as a constitutional right.** Enshrine access to drinking water in the Constitution and shield water resources from privatisation, as Slovenia did in 2016 with Article 70a of its constitution.
- **Energy in the hands of communities.** A legal and financial framework for energy cooperatives and citizen energy communities, fully implementing the EU Renewable Energy Directive (2018/2001), and drawing on Denmark's example — whose 2009 Renewable Energy Act required new wind projects to offer at least 20% ownership to local residents, building one of the world's great wind industries on cooperative foundations. Romanian renewables should enrich Romanian communities, not replicate the extraction model with solar panels.
- **End the forest massacre.** Strict protection for all remaining virgin and old-growth forests; full enforcement of the EU Deforestation Regulation (2023/1115) and Timber Regulation against every actor in the supply chain, however large; a functioning, public timber-tracking system; and criminal liability that reaches the corporations that profit, not only the drivers of the trucks.
- **A Green New Deal for Romania.** A public investment programme — anchored in the Green New Deal for Europe — to insulate homes, modernise heating, and build public transport and rail, prioritising the households in energy poverty first and creating good jobs in every county doing it.

Romanians defended Roșia Montană and won. That victory is the proof and the promise: this country's nature is not for sale, and its people already know how to defend it.
