# DiEM25 Romania — Vision Documents

Working drafts of a localized political vision for [DiEM25](https://diem25.org)
in Romania, written within the movement's Bucharest Collective. They adapt the
six sections of the international [DiEM25 vision](https://diem25.org/vision/)
— economy, politics, culture, ecology, technology and security — to Romanian
realities.

**These are drafts for discussion, not official DiEM25 policy.** They were
drafted with AI assistance, using the reference documents in this repo as
context, and are edited and decided on by humans.

The six drafts live in [`romania/vision/`](romania/vision/):

## The approach

The international DiEM25 vision documents are deliberately broad, leaving each
country room to develop its own proposals. These drafts aim to be more
concrete, while staying honest about their limits:

- **Concrete policies, high-level descriptions.** Each document names real
  policy directions but leaves detailed, costed specifications to future
  democratic deliberation — and says so openly.
- **Every proposal already works somewhere in Europe.** Each one cites the
  actual law, directive or court ruling — from Germany's codetermination to
  Spain's solidarity wealth tax to Slovenia's constitutional right to water —
  so readers can verify for themselves.
- **Empirical.** Claims are backed by verifiable facts and numbers.
- **Reform the EU, don't leave it.** The documents direct anger at corporate
  extraction, not at European integration or the peoples of other countries.
- **Decentralist.** They favor bottom-up forms of economic and political
  democracy — cooperatives, participatory budgeting, citizens' assemblies —
  over anything resembling top-down state control.

The documents are written in English so they can be discussed across DiEM25;
a Romanian translation will follow once the English versions stabilize.

## Repo contents

- `romania/` — the DiEM25 Romania drafts, including the six vision documents
  in `romania/vision/`.
- `international/` — reference material: DiEM25 website pages and PDFs
  converted to markdown.
- `scripts/` — tooling, including the PDF-to-markdown converter
  (`uv run scripts/pdf_to_md.py <file.pdf>`).

## Feedback and adaptation

Feedback from DiEM25 members is welcome — from the Bucharest Collective,
other Romanian cities, or other countries. Feel free to open an issue, or to
fork and adapt these documents for your own collective.
