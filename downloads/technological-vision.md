# Technological Vision

*Our call for a digital commons.*

We envision **a digital commons** in which people fully own their data, platforms whose oligarch owners operate like feudal lords are socialised, commercial services are cheaply bought (ending the ‘free’ services that, currently, turn us into Big Tech’s products) and a **Universal Declaration of Human Rights** that bans all autonomous AI-based weapons while protecting every human’s dignity and right. Our Europe will be:

- **Creative and technologically sovereign,**through nurturing and safeguarding the innovative powers of its people’s imagination.
- **Responsible,**putting its home-grown technologies into the service of solidarity and people, not profit.
